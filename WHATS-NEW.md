# What's new in LocalApply

Changes you can see, newest first. The technical history for developers is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.6.2 · October 22, 2026

- After you install LocalApply, the profile page opens by itself, with a short note on where to start.
- The LocalApply button in the toolbar takes the colour of the palette you chose, and so does the logo in the popup and on the profile page.
- The store pages and the extension's name in your browser are now in all eight languages.

## 0.6.1 · October 22, 2026

- New **Theme** switch at the top of the profile page: **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. System follows your computer's setting, as before; Light and Dark keep LocalApply that way whatever the computer uses. The popup, dropdowns and date pickers follow your choice.

## 0.6.0 · October 22, 2026

- The phone number has three boxes that fill each other in: **Country code**, **Number** and **Full number**. Type +48791532218 into Full number and the code and number appear on their own; change either part and the full number follows.
- New **Sexual orientation** in the voluntary disclosures. Like the others, it stays blank unless you set it and is only used when a form asks.
- On wide screens the profile sits in the middle of the window, with the list of sections to its left.

## 0.5.0 · October 22, 2026

- New **Highest education level** on the profile page, from high school to PhD. LocalApply picks the matching option when a form asks, in any of its eight languages, and also uses it for **Degree** dropdowns that list levels such as Bachelor and Master. An MBA counts as a master's on forms that don't list it.
- Five colour palettes: Forest, Ocean, Plum, Clay and Graphite, each with a dark version. Pick one with the swatches at the top of the profile page; the popup and the message shown after filling use it too.

## 0.4.1 · October 21, 2026

- A security release with no visible changes: the part of the interface toolkit that could write raw HTML into a page is now removed from the extension.

## 0.4.0 · October 20, 2026

- LocalApply speaks your language: the profile page, the popup and the message shown after filling are in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese or Polish. It starts in your browser's language; change it with the **Language** menu at the top of the profile page.
- A new look in light and dark mode, with a warm paper-and-ink palette and new typefaces.
- The profile page reads like one document with numbered sections. An index beside it shows how much of each section is filled in.
- After each change, **All changes saved** briefly turns green with a check mark.

## 0.3.0 · October 16, 2026

- Enter your phone number either as country code and number in two boxes, or as one full number such as +442079460123. Forms that ask either way are filled.
- When a form doesn't show which date order it expects, dates are filled as DD-MM-YYYY.

## 0.2.1 · October 16, 2026

- The Firefox version can now be installed from addons.mozilla.org. If you installed an earlier version by hand, export your profile before updating and import it afterwards.

## 0.2.0 · October 16, 2026

- New **Undo**: in the popup and in the message shown after filling, it puts every filled field back the way it was.
- Outlines differ in line style as well as colour: solid green for filled, dashed amber for questions that need you. The popup explains them.
- The section links and the save status on the profile page stay in view while you scroll.
- Many fixes: visa questions answered with the opposite answer, address line 2 filled with the street, searchable dropdowns that only tried your exact wording, rich text boxes that looked filled but submitted empty, and more.

## 0.1.0 · October 14, 2026

- First release, for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox.
- Save your details once on the profile page: personal details, address, links, salary, notice period, start date, work authorisation, education, CV and cover letter.
- Fill a form with one click from the toolbar, or with **Alt+Shift+F**. LocalApply never submits a form and never ticks consent boxes.
- Recognises form labels in eight languages and works with Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Personio, SmartRecruiters, SAP SuccessFactors and LinkedIn Easy Apply as well as ordinary career pages.
- Questions it can't answer are listed in the popup, where you can save an answer for next time.
