**Free. No account. No subscription. Your data stays on your computer.**

Stop retyping your CV into every job application. LocalApply saves your details once, only in Firefox on your computer, and fills application forms in one click: name, contact details, address, links, salary expectation, availability and your CV. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay. You stay in control: review the form and press Submit yourself.

**Why job seekers use it**

- **Apply faster.** The repetitive part of an application is done in one click, so your time goes into the questions that matter.
- **Keep your data private.** Your profile stays in Firefox on your computer. No account, no server, no AI service, no tracking.
- **Apply in any language.** Recognises form labels in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese and Polish.
- **Make it yours.** The interface speaks eight languages, with five colour palettes and a light or dark theme.
- **Answer each question once.** Save your answer to a recurring question like "How did you hear about us?" and it is filled in next time.

**What it fills**

- Name, email, phone (including country-code pickers), address and date of birth
- LinkedIn, GitHub, Xing and portfolio links
- Salary expectation, notice period, earliest start date and years of experience
- Work authorisation, visa sponsorship and relocation questions
- Spoken languages and their level, driving licence, travel and salary-range questions
- Your cover letter and saved answers, with {company} and {position} replaced by the employer and job title
- Work history and education, one entry per block on forms that list several jobs or schools, and your highest education level
- Several profiles, for example one per language or kind of job, chosen in the popup
- CV and cover letter uploads, and cover letter text

**Where it works**

Greenhouse, Ashby, Personio, Recruitee, Teamtailor and ordinary company career pages, with site-specific handling for Workday, Lever, SmartRecruiters, SAP SuccessFactors and LinkedIn Easy Apply.

**How to use it**

1. Enter your details once on the profile page. It opens by itself after you install LocalApply.
2. Open a job application, click the LocalApply toolbar button and then **Fill this page**, or press **Alt+Shift+F**.
3. Check the result. Filled fields get a solid green outline; required questions it could not answer get a dashed amber outline and are listed so you can finish them. **Undo** puts the fields back.

**Safe by design**

- Never presses Submit or Next, and never ticks consent boxes.
- Leaves fields you have already filled in alone.
- Export your profile to a file, or delete everything with one click.
- A log of the applications you filled stays on your computer too; turn it off, clear it or export it as CSV.

**Why it needs access to all websites**

Job applications are hosted on thousands of employer and recruiting sites, often inside embedded frames, so LocalApply must be able to fill forms on any page. It reads and fills a form only when you ask it to. It makes no network requests.
