Free. No account. No subscription. Your data stays on your computer.

Applying for jobs means typing the same details into every form. LocalApply keeps them in one profile, stored only in your browser, and fills the application you have open with a single click. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay, and no server your CV gets uploaded to. You check the form and press Submit yourself.

WHAT IT FILLS
• Name, email, phone (including country-code pickers), address and date of birth
• LinkedIn, GitHub, Xing and portfolio links
• Salary expectation, notice period, earliest start date and years of experience
• Work authorisation, visa sponsorship and relocation questions
• Spoken languages and their level, driving licence, travel and salary-range questions
• Your cover letter and saved answers, with {company} and {position} replaced by the employer and job title
• Work history and education, one entry per block on forms that list several jobs or schools, and your highest education level
• Several profiles, for example one per language or kind of job, chosen in the popup
• Your CV and cover letter files, and cover letter text
• Answers you type into new questions: LocalApply offers to save them, so the next form gets them
• Any single field you right-click, with one of your details or saved answers

WORKS WHERE YOU APPLY
Works on the application forms of most applicant tracking systems and on ordinary company career pages, including forms inside embedded frames. Some of the most widely used job sites get extra, site-specific handling. Form labels are recognised in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese and Polish.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your details once on the profile page. Installing LocalApply opens a short welcome page that takes you there.
2. On an application, click the toolbar button and then "Fill this page", or press Alt+Shift+F.
3. Filled fields get a solid green outline. Required fields it could not answer get a dashed amber outline and are listed in the popup, where you can save an answer for next time. Changed your mind? Undo puts the fields back. Answers you type in yourself are offered for saving, and a right-click fills any single field.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your profile and documents are stored only in your browser on your computer.
• No account, no server, no AI service, no analytics. The extension makes no network requests.
• It never presses Submit or Next and never ticks consent boxes.
• Fields you have already filled in are left alone.
• Export your profile to a file, or delete everything with one click.
• A log of the applications you filled stays on your computer too, and the popup tells you when you applied to a company before; turn it off, clear it or export it as CSV.

WHY IT ASKS TO READ AND CHANGE DATA ON ALL WEBSITES
Job applications live on thousands of different domains, often inside embedded frames, so LocalApply needs to be able to fill form fields on any page. It reads and fills a page's form only when you ask it to.
